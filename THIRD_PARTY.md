# Assets and dependencies

- The Franka Panda model and meshes under `assets/panda/` come from the course simulation assets. The supplied Apache License 2.0 text is in `assets/panda/LICENSE`.
- The project team supplied both tray designs. `inputs/ball_ballancing_platform.STL` is the revised 233 mm version. `assets/platform/platform_tray_frame.stl` translates that STL into the centered tray frame. The earlier 150 mm source is `inputs/ball balancing platform.STL`.
- Python packages are installed separately from `requirements.txt`. Each retains its own license.
