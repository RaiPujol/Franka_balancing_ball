# Assets and dependencies

- The Franka Panda simulation model and meshes under `assets/panda/` were retained from the course/lab simulation assets. The included `assets/panda/LICENSE` contains the Apache License 2.0 text supplied with those assets.
- `inputs/ball balancing platform.STL` was supplied for this project by the user. `assets/platform/platform_tray_frame.stl` is its translated derivative.
- Python packages listed in `requirements.txt`, including MuJoCo, NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib, ImageIO and Pillow, are separate dependencies with their own licenses; they are not vendored in this folder.
